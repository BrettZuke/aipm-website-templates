## What you sell

The next four decide what your website has pages for and what your chatbot is allowed to say.

**List everything you do***

One per line. Do not be shy about the small jobs.

**Which three do you want more of?***

The ones that make you the most money, or that you enjoy most. These get the most room on the site.

**If you were the customer, what would you type into Google to find a business like yours?***

Three or four goes at it, one per line, exactly as you would type them on your phone. This decides the category your Google listing goes in and the words your pages are built around, and the way you say it beats the way we would guess it.

**What do you not do?**

Jobs you turn down or send elsewhere. This stops the website and the chatbot bringing you work you do not want.

**Prices you are happy to show publicly**

Call-out fee, hourly rate, typical job prices, or from prices. If you would rather show none, write none: we will never invent a price, and your chatbot will never quote one.

## Why you, and the proof

This is the part that sells. It is also the part only you can answer.

**Why do customers pick you over the firm down the road?***

In your own words. If customers have told you why, use their words.

**Do you make any guarantee or promise?**

Workmanship guarantee, fixed prices, no mess, turn up when we say. Anything you stand behind.

**Licences, insurance, accreditations, memberships and awards**

Gas Safe, NICEIC, FENSA, CHAS, DBS, public liability cover, trade bodies, local awards. Anything a careful customer might check.

**A link to your Google reviews**

Open your listing on Google, press Share, and paste the link.

**Paste three things customers have said about you**

Copy them out of Google, Facebook, texts or emails, with a first name and town if you can. Real words beat anything we could write.

**Where are your photos?***

A link to a folder (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) holding photos of your work, your team, your van or shop, and your logo. Before and after shots are worth the most. If you have none, write none and we will tell you exactly what to take on your phone.
