Most driveways fail from below, not on top.

Sunken blocks, ruts and puddles are almost always a thin sub base. We dig down 200mm, lay and compact the stone in layers, and only then put down the surface you picked.

What we lay

Four Surfaces, One Standard Underneath

Whatever goes on top, the base, the edging and the drainage are done the same way.

Block paving driveways

Concrete or clay block in any pattern and colour, with a proper kerb edge and kiln dried sand compacted into every joint.

Resin bound driveways

Smooth, permeable and weed free, laid on an open grade base so rain drains straight through and planning is rarely needed.

Patios and steps

Indian sandstone, porcelain and granite, laid on a full mortar bed with falls away from the house and steps built to proper rises.

Groundwork and drainage

Excavation, sub base, channel drains, soakaways and dropped kerb applications with the council, all handled in house.

How it works

Three Steps, No Surprises

The site visit decides whether the other two go well.

01

Step 01

Site visit

An hour on site, levels taken, drainage checked

We measure, take levels, check where the water goes and ask about the cars and vans that will use it. You see samples of every surface in daylight at your own house.
02

Step 02

Design

A drawn layout and a fixed written price

A drawing of the layout, the pattern and the edging, and one fixed price that includes the dig out, the skips and the drainage. Nothing on it is an estimate.
03

Step 03

The build

Most driveways in five to seven working days

Dig out, sub base compacted in layers, edging set in concrete, then the surface. Your drive is swept, jointed and usable the day we leave, and the guarantee arrives by email.
