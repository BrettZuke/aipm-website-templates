How it goes

First Lesson, Every Lesson, and Test Day

Parents get a short note after every teen lesson, so nobody has to guess how it went.

The first lesson

An empty parking lot, the controls explained properly, and no traffic until you are ready. Most learners are on quiet streets by the end of the first hour.

Every lesson after

Same instructor, and a plan that builds lesson by lesson: turns, lane changes, parking, then highways and night driving. You get a note afterwards on what to practise before next time.

Test day

A warm up lesson on the morning of the road test, our car for the test itself, and your instructor waiting outside when you finish.
