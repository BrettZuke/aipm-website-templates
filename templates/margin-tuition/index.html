How it works

Assessed, planned, taught, reported

  1. 1

    A free assessment

    45 minutes in the subject to find the gaps, and a chat with you about school and goals.

  2. 2

    A written plan

    The grades we would aim for at each mock, and the topics in order, following your school's exam board.

  3. 3

    Weekly sessions

    Small groups of up to four, or one to one, with homework set and marked every week.

  4. 4

    A report every half term

    What your child can do now, what they cannot yet, and what we are doing about it.
