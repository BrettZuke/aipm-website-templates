The small print, in plain English

Representative example. A car at £12,450 with a deposit of £1,245, then 60 monthly payments of £253. Total amount payable £16,425, total cost of credit £3,975, at 12.9% APR representative.

Tollgate Motors is a credit broker, not a lender. We work with a panel of lenders and are paid a commission, which we tell you about before you sign. Finance is subject to status and affordability, and you must be 18 or over.

Miss payments and it will cost you more and may affect your credit file. The car is not yours until the last payment is made.

Before it goes on the pitch

Seven things, every car, no exceptions

  1. 1

    History check

    HPI clear on finance, theft, write offs and mileage. You get the certificate with the car.

  2. 2

    A full service

    Oil, filters, plugs where they are due, and the stamp in the book.

  3. 3

    Fresh MOT

    Twelve months from the day you drive away, not from when it came in.

  4. 4

    Brakes and tyres

    Anything under 3 mm is replaced, and pads under 4 mm go too.

  5. 5

    Two keys where we can

    If there is only one, we tell you and knock the cost of a spare off the price.

  6. 6

    Six month warranty

    Parts and labour, at our workshop or any garage you trust.

  7. 7

    Fourteen days to change your mind

    Bring it back within a fortnight, under 500 miles, and we will refund it.
