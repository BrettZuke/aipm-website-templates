How it works

From the first visit to the first film

  1. 01

    A free design visit

    We measure the room, listen to it, and ask how you actually watch and listen.

  2. 02

    Drawings and a fixed price

    A plan like the one above with every speaker and cable run placed, and one price that does not move.

  3. 03

    Cables first, kit second

    Cables go in before the plaster. We come back to fit, then calibrate the sound with a meter and by ear.

  4. 04

    Handover and aftercare

    We show everyone in the house the keypad, and fix most problems remotely the same day.
