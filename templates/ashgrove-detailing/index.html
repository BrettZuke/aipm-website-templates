Before You Book

A maintenance detail is two to three hours. A full interior and exterior with a single stage machine polish is a full day, and a two stage correction on neglected paint can be two. We tell you which one your car needs after we have seen a photo, not after we have arrived.

Neither. The van carries its own water tank, a generator and its own lighting, so we can work on a driveway, a street or an office car park without plugging into anything of yours.

Only once the paint has been corrected. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so putting one on swirled paint means paying to preserve the swirls for three years. If your paint is not ready we will say so and quote the correction first.

Light rain is fine for an interior and we work under a canopy. Heavy rain or anything below about four degrees stops paint correction, because the products stop behaving, so we move you rather than rush it. You are never charged for a move we made.

A maintenance detail starts at 85 pounds, a full interior at 160, and a single stage correction with protection from 380 depending on the size and condition of the car. You get a fixed number before we come out.
