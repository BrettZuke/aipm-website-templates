How it works

Three Steps, No Surprises

The first one is the one that decides whether the other two go well.

01

Step 01

Survey

Twenty minutes, every cupboard, in person or on video

Twenty minutes in person or over video. We look in every cupboard, the loft and the shed, ask about access at both ends, and note the piano before it becomes a problem on the day.
02

Step 02

Plan

A written fixed price with the van, the crew and the hours

A written quote with the van size, the crew size and the hours, plus a packing plan if you want one. Nothing on it says estimate and nothing on it moves later.
03

Step 03

Move day

The same crew, wrapped in the room, beds back up before we go

The same crew who surveyed it turns up to do it. Everything is wrapped before it leaves a room, the van is loaded in unloading order, and the beds go back up before we leave.
