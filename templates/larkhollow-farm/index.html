The whole farm, and everything on the plan

Every wedding here has the farm to itself from 10 am to 11 pm, and all of this comes with it.

  • The whole farm

    One wedding a day, so the Grove, the Terrace and the barn are yours alone.

  • Tables and chairs for 180

    Round and farm tables, cross back chairs and white linens.

  • A coordinator, start to finish

    Dana plans the timeline with you, runs the rehearsal and runs the day.

  • The Cottage

    For getting ready from 10 am, and for the two of you that night.

  • The bar and bartenders

    Bring your own drinks, and our licensed bartenders pour them.

  • Set up and clear up

    We lay out the barn to your floor plan, and clear it all away after.

  • The rain plan

    The Chapel is held for you on the day, at no extra cost.

  • Parking and a shuttle

    Room for 100 cars, and a golf cart from the lot to the Grove.
