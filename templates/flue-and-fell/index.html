Is your wood ready?

Twenty percent, or it stays in the store

Split a log and push a moisture meter into the fresh face. Wet wood hisses, smokes and coats your flue with tar, which is how most chimney fires start.

Since 2021, logs sold in small bags in England must be 20% moisture or less. Ours are kiln dried to about 15%.
