How it works

Four steps, and none of them are yours

Send the package, tell us when, and the next thing you handle is a clean scan-back in your inbox.

1

Send the package

Email the documents or drop them in your portal. We print two sets on legal and letter, colour where the lender needs it, at our cost.

2

We confirm the appointment

We call your signer within the hour, confirm their ID and text you the time. If they cannot find their driver's licence we learn that today, not at the table.

3

We meet and notarise

At their kitchen table, their office or the hospital, wherever they actually are. Every signature and initial in order, journal entered, nothing left blank.

4

Scan-backs before we drive off

The whole package scanned from the car and emailed to you, then dropped at the shipping label the same afternoon. You get the tracking number too.

The questions we get every week

We travel to your signer and witness them sign, check their identification, apply the seal and record the act in a journal. A notary confirms who signed and that they signed willingly. We cannot tell you what the document means, and anyone who does is not acting as a notary.

A current, government issued photo ID with a signature and a physical description: a California driver's licence, a state ID, a US passport or a military ID. Expired is fine in California only if it was issued within the last five years. If they have nothing, two credible witnesses who know them can identify them instead.
