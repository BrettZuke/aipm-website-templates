How we work

Inspect, Treat, Guarantee

Two things decide whether a treatment still works in three months, and neither of them is the spray.

Find it before you treat it

Every visit starts with a walk round the outside and every room: entry points, moisture, droppings, trails. We seal what we can and show you what we found, because a treatment without an inspection is a guess.

Safe once it is dry

Targeted, low toxicity products applied where pests travel rather than sprayed across the room. Pets and kids are back in once it is dry, usually inside the hour, and we tell you exactly what was used.
