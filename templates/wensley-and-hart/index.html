What to do first

One step at a time

What happens in the first hours and days depends on where they died. Choose below, and take it at your own pace.

Where did they die?

Was it expected?

You can call us at any point, even just to ask what comes next.

0113 496 0719
  1. 1

    Call their GP, or 111 out of hours

    A doctor or nurse comes to confirm the death. There is no rush. Sit with them for as long as you need.

  2. 2

    Call us, whenever you are ready

    Day or night, a person answers. We come to the house, usually within two hours, and bring them into our care.

  3. 3

    The medical certificate

    Their doctor writes the medical certificate and a medical examiner reviews it. This usually takes a few days, and it is sent to the registrar for you.

  4. 4

    Register the death within 5 days

    Book an appointment at the register office. The registrar can tell most government departments for you, through the Tell Us Once service.

  5. 5

    Plan the funeral with us

    At your home or ours, in your own time. You have a written estimate before anything is decided.
