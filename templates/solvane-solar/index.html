Most of our customers cut their APS or SRP bill by seventy to ninety percent. We show you the numbers from your last twelve bills before you sign anything, not a national average.

The crew is usually on your roof for one or two days. The whole process, including design, the permit and the utility switching you on, takes about four to six weeks.

The federal credit for homeowner owned systems is set by current law, which has changed recently. We check what applies to your install on the day you sign and show it on the quote, rather than promising a number.

We check the roof on the survey. If it has less than ten years left, we tell you to replace it first, and we remove and reinstall panels later at a fixed price if you ever need a new roof.
