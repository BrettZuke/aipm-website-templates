Our Services

What We Build

From a single gate to a full backyard, built by the same crew from the first post to the final stain.

01

Fences

Cedar privacy fences

Six or eight foot, board on board

Western red cedar on steel or pressure treated posts, set in concrete below the frost line so the first hard freeze does not heave them. From 42 dollars a foot, installed.

02

Decks

Composite and hardwood decks

Framed to code, finished to last

Trex, TimberTech or ipe on a joist frame built to code with the right spacing and flashing. Designed with you, drawn up for the permit, and finished in about two weeks.

03

Shade

Pergolas and covered patios

Shade that survives the wind

Cedar or aluminum pergolas, louvered roofs and covered patios, engineered for Front Range wind and snow load and tied into the deck frame properly.

Repairs

Gates, repairs and staining

Sagging gates and tired boards

Rehung and squared gates, replaced posts and boards, and a proper clean and stain so a ten year old fence gets another ten.
