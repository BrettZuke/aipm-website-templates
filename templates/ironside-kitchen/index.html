Catering

We will bring the whole thing to you

Weddings, film sets, warehouse lunches and street parties. One truck, one crew, one invoice, and a menu you sign off before the day.

Weddings and parties

Late night tacos after the speeches, or the whole dinner off the truck. We hold the date with a deposit and serve up to 200 without a queue forming.

Drop-off lunches

Trays delivered hot to the office or the job site, labelled, with the vegetarian count you gave us. Twelve people minimum, ordered by 9am for the same day.

Book the whole truck

Four hours on your street, in your car park or at the venue. You pick three mains and two sides, we bring power, water and the permit paperwork. Portland metro, no travel charge inside 20 miles.
