The tunnel

Why This One Does Not Scratch

Three things separate a wash you can run weekly from one that puts swirls in your clear coat.

1

Foam, not bristles

Closed cell foam sheds grit with the water instead of holding it between fibres and dragging it down the panel. That single material choice is most of the difference between a weekly wash and a swirled clear coat.

See the plans
2

Reverse osmosis rinse

The last rinse runs on reverse osmosis water with the minerals stripped out, so there is nothing left behind as it dries. No spots, and nobody has to towel your car at the exit.

See the plans
3

Vacuums that actually pull

Fourteen bays on a dedicated pump, free with any wash and no timer on them. The vacuums are where most washes quietly cut costs, so ours are the part we over spec.
