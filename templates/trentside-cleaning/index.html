The Questions We Get Asked

Straight answers, including the ones that talk you out of the wrong job.

We drop the pressure and use a rotary surface cleaner, which holds the water at a fixed distance across the whole slab so you get no stripes and no pitting. Then the joints get kiln-dried sand brushed back in, because an unsanded drive is exactly what lets the blocks rock and the weeds back through.

No, and we would talk you out of anyone who offers to. A jet strips the surface off a concrete tile and can push water up under the laps. Roofs get a soft wash instead: low pressure, a biocide that kills the moss at the root, and months of it carrying on working so the growth goes brittle and weathers off on its own.

Most driveways around Nottingham land between 180 and 400 pounds, depending on the size, the surface and how far gone it is. Send a photo through the form and you get one fixed number back the same day rather than a range and a site visit.

Borders get sheeted and the planting gets watered down first so nothing takes up the run-off. At the end we flush the downpipes and the gully, then the silt goes in the van with us. You should not be sweeping up after we have gone.

A soft washed roof usually holds for three to four years. A patio or a drive is closer to eighteen months to two years in this part of the country, and sooner if it sits under trees or faces north and never gets the sun on it.
