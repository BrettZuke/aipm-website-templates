How it goes

First Visit, Every Visit, and What Lands in Your Inbox

No surprises, and nothing that needs you to be home.

The first visit

A full water test, a look at every piece of equipment, and an honest read on the plaster. If the pool needs a drain or a repair before a route makes sense, we say so rather than signing you up to mask it.

Every visit after

Same tech, same day of the week. Skim, brush, baskets, vacuum where it needs it, then chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium, cyanuric acid, salt and phosphates all tested and corrected on the spot.

What gets sent

Every reading, what we added, and a photo of the water before we left. If something needs watching you hear about it that week, not in three months once it has turned into a repair.
