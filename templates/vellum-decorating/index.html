How we work

Prep, Paint, Put Back

Two things decide whether a decorating job still looks right in two years, and neither of them is the colour you picked.

Trade paint, not the tin on offer

It covers in two coats instead of three, it does not flash when the light moves across the wall, and it takes a wipe down without polishing itself shiny in patches.

Put back the way we found it

Sheets down before a tin is opened, sockets off rather than cut around, edges caulked, and the room back together on the last afternoon instead of the following week.
