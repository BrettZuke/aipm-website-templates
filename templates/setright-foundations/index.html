Push piers

Through the clay, down to rock

Kansas City clay swells in wet springs and shrinks in dry summers, and footings ride along with it. A pier ignores the clay and carries the house on limestone instead.

  1. 01

    Dig beside the footing

    A small pit at each pier spot, with the lawn cut out and set aside.

  2. 02

    Drive it with the house

    Steel sections are pushed down using the weight of the house itself, until they hit rock.

  3. 03

    Test, lift, and backfill

    Each pier is load tested, the corner lifted back toward level, and the lawn put back.

From $1,950 a pier. Most homes need six to ten.
