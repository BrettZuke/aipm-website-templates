FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions people actually ask at the counter.

A standard wash is 28 minutes and a dryer cycle is 35, so most people are in and out inside ninety minutes. The big 60 pound washers do about four normal loads at once if you are trying to save a trip.

Before ten in the morning and after eight at night, any day. Sunday afternoon is the busiest three hours of the week and Monday evening is a close second, so avoid both if you can.

Drop the bag at the counter, we weigh it, and it is ready the same day if it is in before eleven. One sixty five a pound, minimum ten pounds, and everything is washed separately from other customers.

Every machine takes tap, card or coin, and the change machine is filled twice a day. If a machine takes your money the attendant refunds it on the spot rather than asking you to fill in a form.
