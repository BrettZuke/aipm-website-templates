How it runs

What Happens and When

A new kitchen or bathroom has an order to it. Get it wrong and the tiler waits for the plumber who waits for the electrician.

Week 0, the design visit

We measure, listen and draw. You get plans, elevations and a fixed price, and nothing is ordered until you have signed them.

Week 1, strip out

Old units, tiles and ceiling down, walls back to the brick, and the skip gone before the weekend.

Week 1, first fix

Plumbing and electrics moved to the new layout, pipes pressure tested, and every socket where the drawings say it goes.

Week 2, floors and walls

Walls skimmed flat, underfloor heating laid, and the floor down and level before a single unit goes in.

Week 2, the fit

Cabinets levelled to the millimetre, worktops templated on site, and doors hung and adjusted until every gap matches.

Week 3, worktops and tiling

Stone worktops fitted from the template, splashbacks and walls tiled, and every appliance connected and tested.

Week 3, handover

A deep clean, a walk round with your project manager, and every guarantee and manual in one folder.
