Fitting day

Upstairs by lunchtime

Most straight stairlifts are fitted in one morning, by two of our own fitters. This is how the morning goes.

  1. 9:00

    Dust sheets down

    Ade and Tom arrive, lay dust sheets and check your stairs against the survey.

  2. 9:20

    The rail goes on the stairs

    Small brackets hold it to the treads, so nothing is drilled into your wall.

  3. 10:30

    The chair goes on

    Seat, swivel and safety belt fitted, and the batteries put on charge.

  4. 11:15

    You ride it first

    We go up and down with you, then show you and a family member every control.

  5. 12:00

    Tidy, and done

    Every scrap goes with us. The first year of servicing is already included.
