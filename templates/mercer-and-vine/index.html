Your first visit

Three Steps to the Right Chair

New clients get a little longer on the first visit, at no extra cost.

1

Book online or call

Pick salon or barbers, choose a stylist or let us match you, and tell us what you are after in a line. We book colour clients in for a free patch test first.

The consultation
