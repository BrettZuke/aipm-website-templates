How we design

Every garden starts as a drawing like this

A real plan for a 14 m garden behind a Victorian terrace in Battersea. Tap a number to see what it is, why it is there, and what it cost.

Scale 1:100

Elspeth Road, SW11Garden plan, revision C

  1. Laid level with the kitchen floor, in the same pale stone, so the room carries straight on outside.

  2. Real turf on 100 mm of new topsoil, with a steel mowing edge so it stays crisp.

  3. Grasses, salvias and alliums, planted for colour from May to October, with evergreens to hold it through winter.

  4. White stems and dappled shade over the lawn, without taking light from the kitchen.

  5. Insulated and wired, 4.4 by 3 m, and kept under 2.5 m tall so it normally needs no planning permission.

  6. Sawn stone through gravel, so you can walk to the room in slippers after rain.

  7. A round of setts with a curved oak bench, tucked in the far corner for autumn evenings.

  8. Trained trees on clear stems, so the windows behind disappear by the first summer.
