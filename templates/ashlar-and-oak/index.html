Old walls are never straight

Scribed to the wall, not filled with silicone

Edinburgh tenements and Georgian flats lean, bow and slope. Every Ashlar and Oak piece has a scribe edge, a strip left long in the workshop and cut on site to follow your wall exactly, so there is no gap and no bead of filler.

The workshop in Leith

Drawn, made and fitted by the same four people

  1. Week 1

    Survey and drawing

    Measured to the millimetre, drawn, and agreed with you line by line.

  2. Weeks 2 and 3

    Made in Leith

    Cut, jointed and dry fitted in the workshop before a panel leaves.

  3. Week 3

    Sprayed

    Painted in our spray booth, so the finish is flawless and your house stays clean.

  4. Week 4

    Fitted

    Carried up, scribed to the walls and hung, usually in a day or two.
