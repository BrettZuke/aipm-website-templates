Through the year

What Happens and When

A lawn does different things in different months. The plan follows the grass rather than the invoice date.

March, the first cut

High blade, no stripes, nothing taken off the crown. The first cut of the year is about waking the lawn up rather than making it look sharp.

December, hedges and hard pruning

The month the mower stays in the van. Hedges get their shape back and anything that needs cutting hard comes down while it is dormant.

July, drought watch

We raise the blade and cut less often. A lawn going brown in July is doing exactly what it should, and scalping it is what kills it.

May, weekly from here

Growth doubles and a fortnightly cut starts taking too much off at once. Most gardens move to weekly until the end of June.

April, spring feed

A granular feed and a selective weed while the grass is growing hard enough to use it. Do it too early and it just feeds the moss.

September, scarify and overseed

The most useful week of the year. The thatch comes out, seed goes down into the gaps, and the lawn goes into winter thick instead of bare.

October, last cut and leaves

One final cut a little higher than usual, then leaves off the lawn every visit until they stop falling. Leaves left on grass are what kills patches over winter.
