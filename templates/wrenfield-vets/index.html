    Is it an emergency?

    Call us straight away, day or night, if your pet

    • is struggling to breathe
    • has collapsed or had a seizure
    • has a swollen belly and keeps retching without bringing anything up
    • has eaten something toxic: chocolate, grapes or raisins, xylitol sweeteners, rat poison, or lilies if they are a cat
    • is bleeding and it has not stopped after five minutes of firm pressure
    • is a male cat straining to pee
    • has been hit by a car, even if they seem fine

    New to the family

    Their first year, step by step

    1. 8 weeks

      First vaccination and a full health check. By law, your puppy should already be microchipped.

    2. 10 to 12 weeks

      Second vaccination. A week later they can go out on walks and meet other dogs.

    3. 4 months

      Puppy weigh-in and a chat about training, teeth and insurance.

    4. 6 months

      Neutering conversation. The right time depends on breed and size.

    5. 12 months

      First booster, and they move onto the adult health plan.

    The first year on our puppy and kitten plan is £12.50 a month, vaccinations included.
