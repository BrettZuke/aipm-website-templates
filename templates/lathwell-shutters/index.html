How it works

Measured once, made for your window

  1. 1

    A free home visit

    We bring real samples, look at the light in each room and measure every window to the millimetre.

  2. 2

    Your price on the day

    A written quote before we leave, with no pressure and nothing to sign then and there.

  3. 3

    Made to measure

    Shutters take six to eight weeks to make, blinds about two.

  4. 4

    Fitted in a morning

    Most rooms take two or three hours, and we take every scrap of packaging away.
