03The paperwork

Four things a cheap quote leaves to you

Planning

Planning or permitted development

We check your house's permitted development rights, draw the plans and submit the application or a lawful development certificate.

Building regs

Building regulations

Full plans approval before we dig, an inspection at every stage, and the completion certificate at the end.

Party wall

Party wall notices

Sharing a wall or digging near next door's foundations brings in the Party Wall Act. We serve the notices and talk to the neighbours first.

Structural

Structural calculations

Our structural engineer sizes every steel beam and lintel, and the calculations go in with the building control pack.
