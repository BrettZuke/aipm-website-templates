FAQ

Before You Call

Including the ones people ask at three in the morning.

Sudden water damage, like a burst pipe or a failed washing machine hose, usually is. Slow leaks and flood water often are not. We read your policy with you on the first visit and bill the insurer directly for anything they cover.

Three to five days for most rooms. We take moisture readings every day and the equipment only leaves when the numbers say the structure is dry, not when it feels dry.

Most insured claims cost you only your deductible. For uninsured work, emergency extraction starts at 350 dollars and a typical room dry out is 1,200 to 2,500 dollars, priced in writing before the equipment goes in.

Rarely. We section off the affected rooms with containment and you live in the rest of the house. If mold or sewage is involved we will tell you straight away whether it is safe to stay.
