Check your BMI

Where you are starting, in plain numbers

Move the sliders. We show your BMI, a healthy range for your height, and whether prescription medicine is usually considered.

BMI is a rough screening number. It does not measure body fat, which is why we scan. This is general information, not medical advice.

30.7Obesity, class 1

  • A healthy weight range for 5 ft 6 in is 115 to 154 lb.
  • 5 percent of your weight is 10 lb. The CDC notes that losing 5 to 10 percent can improve blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar.
  • Prescription weight loss medicine may be an option for you. Your provider decides at your first visit, after your consult and labs.
