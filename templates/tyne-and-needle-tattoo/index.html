How it works

From an idea to a healed tattoo

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea

    By email or in the studio. Bring references, or nothing at all, and we will talk it through.

  2. 2

    A drawing, then a date

    Your artist draws it and sends it over. A 50 pound deposit holds the day and comes off the price.

  3. 3

    The session

    Stencil, check it in the mirror, then we tattoo. Eat first, bring a drink, take breaks whenever you want.

  4. 4

    Healing

    Written aftercare, a check in at two weeks, and free touch ups within six months if it needs one.

We tattoo over 18s only and ask for photo ID every time, even if we know you. That is the law, and we do not bend it.
