Regular home clean

Weekly or fortnightly, two to three hours depending on the house. Kitchen, bathrooms, floors, surfaces and beds changed if you leave the linen out.

One off deep clean

Skirtings, inside the oven, behind the appliances, window tracks and the tops of doors. Usually a full day and usually the first visit before a regular clean starts.

End of tenancy

Inventory standard, with a photo report for the agent and a free return visit if anything gets queried in the first seven days.

After the builders

Dust removal in stages, because one pass never gets it. Two visits a fortnight apart is almost always cheaper than three of ours in a week.

Oven and appliances

Dip tank for the racks and trays, so it is done properly rather than sprayed and wiped. Booked as an add on to any regular clean.

Products and kit

All included and all fragrance free by default. If you want your own products used instead, leave them out and we will use those.

Why us

Why the Same Pair Matters

Three things that are only possible because nobody here is a gig worker.

1

Nobody gets shown round twice

The pair who cleaned last week knows where the hoover lives, which cupboard sticks and that the spare room is not to be touched. That is most of what people mean when they say a cleaner is good.

2

Employed, not self employed

Everyone is on our payroll above the real living wage, insured, DBS checked and covered for holiday and sickness. It costs more and it is the reason people stay for years.

3

An hourly price, published

Twenty two pounds an hour with a two hour minimum, products included. No booking fee, no card fee, and no charge if you cancel more than a day out.

How it starts

Three Steps and You Stop Thinking About It

The first visit is the only one that needs you to be around.

1

Tell us the house

Bedrooms, bathrooms and roughly how it is kept. That is enough to price the hours honestly, and we would rather over estimate than turn up short.
2

Meet the pair

They come round once with you, walk the house, and agree the checklist. After that it is a key safe or a doorstep handover and you never need to be in.
3

Same day, same pair

Same slot every week or fortnight. Change the checklist any time in the app, and if we ever have to send somebody different you hear about it first.
