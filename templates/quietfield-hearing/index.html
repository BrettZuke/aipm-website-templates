Two-minute hearing check

How are your ears doing?

Pop on headphones, find a quiet room, and tap when you hear each tone. You will see the result drawn the way an audiologist draws it.

  1. Use headphones or earphones, and sit somewhere quiet.
  2. Play the test sound and set your volume so it is clear but comfortable.
  3. Then start. Each tone begins silent and slowly gets louder. Tap as soon as you hear it.

A rough check, not a diagnosis. Your device and its volume affect the result. A full hearing test with one of our audiologists is free.
