Cavity walls

Filled in a day, and you cannot tell from the street

  1. 01

    We look inside first

    One small hole and a borescope camera show whether your cavity is clean, dry and right for filling. About one house in ten is not, and we tell you so.

  2. 02

    Holes in the mortar

    We drill 22 mm holes in the mortar lines, about a metre apart, and never through the bricks.

  3. 03

    The wall is filled

    Mineral wool is blown in under pressure until every part of the cavity is full, corners included.

  4. 04

    Made good

    Each hole is filled with mortar matched to yours. By teatime the wall looks as it did.
