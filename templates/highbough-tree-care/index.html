Is your tree protected?

We check the council map before we quote

TPO

Tree Preservation Order

Most work needs the council's consent first. We make the application for you, and the council usually decides within 8 weeks.

6 weeks

Conservation area

We give the council six weeks' notice for any tree with a trunk over 7.5 cm across, measured 1.5 m up from the ground.

Neither

No protection
