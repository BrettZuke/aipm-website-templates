What we treat

Most of What Hurts Has a Mechanical Cause

Three things walk through the door most weeks. If it is something we should not be treating, we tell you on the first visit and send you to the person who should.

Back and neck pain

Desk necks, lifting backs and the stiffness that is worse in the morning. We find what is actually moving badly, treat it hands on, and give you the two exercises that stop it coming back.

Sports and running injuries

Knees, ankles, hamstrings and the niggle that stops you at mile six. Assessed properly, taped if it helps, and a return plan built around your next event rather than a blanket six weeks off.

Shoulders, hips and knees

Frozen shoulders, hip pain on the stairs, and rehab before and after surgery. Measured at the start so you can see it improving, not just feel it.
